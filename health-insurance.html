GOOD QUESTIONS TO ASK

Health insurance, explained simply.

A few useful starting points. Always check the latest policy wording and insurer terms for your specific plan.

Is employer-provided health insurance enough?

It can be useful, but check the sum insured, covered members, exclusions and what happens when you change or leave your job. A personal policy may help provide continuity, subject to its terms.

What is a waiting period?

It is a period during which certain illnesses or treatments are not covered. The length and applicability vary by policy and type of condition.

Can I get health insurance if I have diabetes or high blood pressure?

Some insurers may consider applications with pre-existing conditions, subject to underwriting, disclosures, medical tests, waiting periods, exclusions and applicable terms. Approval is not guaranteed.

What is the difference between a top-up and a super top-up?

Both use a deductible. A top-up generally applies it to an individual claim, while a super top-up generally considers eligible claims together during the policy period. Check the exact wording of each product.

Does cashless mean every hospital bill is fully covered?

No. Cashless treatment is subject to network status, pre-authorisation, policy limits, exclusions, co-payments and other terms. Non-payable items may still be your responsibility.
