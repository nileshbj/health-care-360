COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Which business insurance does my organisation need?

It depends on your sector, operations, assets, workforce, contracts and risk exposures. A needs discussion can help identify what should be reviewed; the insurer determines terms and acceptance.

Can one organisation need more than one policy?

Yes. Property, employee, transit, project and liability exposures are different. The appropriate combination depends on the organisation and the terms available from insurers.

Can you discuss requirements for a Nashik-based business?

Yes. Enquiries from businesses in Nashik district are welcome. Any service, placement or support will be subject to authorisation, insurer arrangements and availability.
