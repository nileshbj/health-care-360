LIFE INSURANCE OPTIONS

Protection for the life you are building.

Start with the purpose of the plan. Different products solve different needs, and policy terms should always be reviewed carefully.

01 · PROTECTION

Term Life Insurance

Pure life protection designed to provide a stated benefit to nominees if the insured person dies during the policy term, subject to policy conditions.

02 · SAVINGS

Traditional Savings Plans

Understand guaranteed or participating savings-linked plans, including premiums, policy duration, benefits, exclusions and surrender conditions.

03 · MARKET-LINKED

ULIPs

Explore unit-linked plans only after understanding market risk, charges, fund choices, lock-in rules and the life cover component.

04 · FUTURE

Retirement & Pension Planning

Review pension and annuity options in the context of retirement income needs, payout choices, liquidity and applicable terms.

05 · EXTRA COVER

Riders & Add-on Benefits

Understand optional benefits such as accidental death, critical illness or premium waiver, where available and subject to definitions and exclusions.

06 · REVIEW

Existing Policy Review

Review what you already own: cover amount, nominee details, premium obligations, policy status, exclusions and whether it still fits your needs.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what you are choosing.

How much life cover should I consider?

There is no universal amount. Consider income replacement, dependants, outstanding loans, future goals, existing assets and current insurance. A needs-based calculation is a better starting point than a fixed multiple alone.

What is the difference between term insurance and savings plans?

Term insurance is primarily designed for life protection over a chosen period. Savings-linked products combine insurance with a savings or investment structure and have different costs, benefits and conditions.

Should I buy a policy only for tax benefits?

Tax treatment can change and depends on the product and applicable law. Protection needs, affordability, policy terms and suitability should come first. Seek current tax advice where required.

What should I check before signing?

Read the benefit illustration and policy wording, understand exclusions, premium duration, surrender or discontinuance terms, nominee details and disclosures. Ask questions before making a decision.
